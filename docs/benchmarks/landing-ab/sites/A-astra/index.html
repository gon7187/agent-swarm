A FEW CLEAN ANSWERS.

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little clarity before your clean slate.

What floors can FLOE One clean?+

FLOE One is made for sealed hard floors: hardwood, tile, vinyl, and laminate. It isn’t designed for carpets, rugs, unsealed wood, or outdoor surfaces. Always follow your flooring manufacturer’s care advice.

Does it really vacuum and mop at once?+

Yes. Suction lifts dry debris and liquid while the roller washes with fresh water. Everything is collected in the dirty-water tank. Very large debris should be picked up by hand first.

How long does the battery last?+

Up to 40 minutes in Eco mode, or around 25 minutes in Max mode. Runtime depends on the floor and mess. A full charge takes approximately 4 hours on the included dock.

What does “self-cleaning” actually mean?+

Place FLOE on its dock and press the cleaning button. A 2-minute fresh-water rinse flushes the roller and internal channel, followed by a 30-minute air-drying cycle. You’ll still need to empty and rinse the dirty-water tank after each use.

Can I use my usual floor cleaner?+

Use FLOE’s low-foam solution or plain water. Other detergents can create excess foam or damage the machine. Your first 500 ml bottle is included; replacement bottles are optional.

What if FLOE isn’t right for my home?+

You have 60 days from delivery to try it. Return it with its accessories for a full refund and a prepaid US return label. Your 2-year limited warranty covers manufacturing defects; normal wear and misuse are excluded.
