FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Our support team replies in under 5 minutes, 8am–10pm.

Which floors can I use it on?

All sealed hard floors: tile, stone, vinyl, laminate, sealed hardwood and LVP. It also handles area rugs up to 5 mm pile in dry-pickup mode. It is not designed for wall-to-wall carpet.

Do I still need a regular vacuum?

For hard floors, no — Glide 3 picks up dry debris like crumbs, dust and pet hair on its own. If your home is mostly carpet, keep your vacuum for those rooms.

Is it safe for hardwood?

Yes. SmartSense meters water flow and the squeegee leaves floors nearly dry, so sealed hardwood never sits wet. There's also an “Eco” mode that uses 40% less water.

Do I have to use the Tidewell cleaning solution?

No. Plain water works great. Any low-foam floor cleaner is fine too — just avoid bleach or high-foam soaps.

How long do rollers and filters last?

We recommend replacing the roller every 6 months and the HEPA filter every 3–6 months. Replacements are $19 and $9, or free with the Home and Max bundles for the first year.

What if I don't love it?

Use it for 60 nights. If it's not for you, we'll send a free prepaid label and refund every cent — no restocking fee, no questions.
