How it works

Clean water in. Dirty water out. Never mixed.

    Spray

    Clean water from the top tank is fed evenly across the roller — never onto the floor in puddles.

    Scrub & lift

    The roller turns at 520 rpm while a scraper squeezes it dry. Suction pulls the dirty water and debris straight up into a separate tank.

  3. 3

    Self-wash

FAQ

Questions? Good.

Still unsure? Talk to our floor-care team at hello@ondine.example.

Is it safe on hardwood floors?

Yes, on sealed hardwood, laminate and engineered wood. The roller is damp, not wet, and the floor dries in about two minutes. Use Eco mode for wood — it uses 30% less water. Don't use it on unsealed or waxed wood.

Can it clean carpets or rugs?

The Glide S1 is built for hard floors. It can pass over thin, low-pile rugs in Dry mode, but it isn't a carpet cleaner. Most owners keep a small stick vacuum for rugs.

How often do I empty the dirty-water tank?

After every session — it takes about 20 seconds. The tank lifts out with one hand, has a wide mouth for rinsing, and the filter is dishwasher-safe.

What do replacement parts cost?

HydroRoll™ brushes are $24 for two and last about 6 months each. HEPA filters are $14 for two. You can subscribe and save 15%, and cancel whenever you like.

Does it work with pet hair?

Yes. A comb inside the head strips long hair from the roller before it wraps, and the self-wash flushes it into the dirty tank. The Complete bundle adds a Pet-Odour Formula.

How does the 60-night trial work?

Use it for up to 60 nights from delivery. If you don't love it, request a return from your account. We'll email a free pickup label and refund you within 5 business days of receiving it.

Is the battery replaceable?

Yes. The battery clicks out of the handle without tools, and a spare costs $89. It's covered by the full 3-year warranty.
